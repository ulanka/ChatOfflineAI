About the app

ChatOfflineAI is a free iOS chat app that runs supported AI models directly on your device, including local models such as Gemma 4, Llama, and Qwen.

  • Works offline after the required models are downloaded or otherwise available on your device
  • Processes chats locally on your device
  • Does not collect your prompts, messages, or AI responses
  • Does not require an account
  • Does not show ads

Model downloads

The app may download model files from Hugging Face so the models can run locally on your device. Downloading a model requires an internet connection. ChatOfflineAI does not send your prompts, chat messages, or AI responses to Hugging Face during model downloads.

Voluntary support purchases

The app may include optional consumable in-app purchases that let you support development. These purchases do not unlock extra features and only add stars on the app's main page.

  • USD 0.99 support purchase adds 1 star
  • USD 2.99 support purchase adds 3 stars
  • USD 4.99 support purchase adds 5 stars

Stars from multiple support purchases are added together on the device where they are stored. Because stars are stored locally, they may not persist if you delete the app, reinstall it, reset your device, or use the app on another device.

Before reporting a problem

  • Make sure you are using the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart the app
  • Check that your device has enough free storage and memory for local AI models
  • For purchase issues, check your Apple purchase history or App Store account

How to report an issue

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include:

  • Clear steps to reproduce the problem
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version
  • The local model you were using, if relevant
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording, if helpful

Privacy

ChatOfflineAI does not collect personal data, does not use ads, and does not track users across apps or websites. Chat content is processed locally on your device after the required model is available.
