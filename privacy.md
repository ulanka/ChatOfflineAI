# Privacy Policy - ChatOfflineAI

**Effective date:** June 2026

ChatOfflineAI ("the App") is operated by the developer Ulan Korabay ("we", "us", "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how privacy works in ChatOfflineAI, an iOS app that lets you chat with local AI models such as Gemma 4, Llama, and Qwen. The App works offline after the required models are downloaded or otherwise available on your device.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect personal data through the App.

The App does not require an account, and we do not collect:

* Your name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Chat messages
* Prompts
* AI responses
* Files or photos
* Location
* Contacts
* Advertising identifiers
* Analytics or tracking data

## 2. Offline Processing

ChatOfflineAI is designed to work offline after the required models are downloaded or otherwise available on your device. Your prompts and AI responses are processed locally on your device.

We do not operate a server that receives your chat content, and we do not use your chats to train AI models.

## 3. Model Downloads

The App may download model files from Hugging Face so the models can run locally on your device.

Downloading models requires an internet connection. During a model download, Hugging Face may receive basic technical information needed to deliver the file, such as your IP address, request metadata, download time, and device or browser-like network information. ChatOfflineAI does not send your prompts, chat messages, or AI responses to Hugging Face during model downloads.

After a model is downloaded and available on your device, chat generation runs locally.

## 4. Local App Data

The App may store information locally on your device so the App can function, such as:

* Chat history, if the App provides local history
* App settings
* Downloaded or bundled model files
* The number of support stars shown in the App

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it.

If you delete the App, reinstall it, reset your device, or use another device, locally stored information may be deleted or unavailable. We cannot recover local chat history, settings, models, or stars.

## 5. Voluntary Support Purchases

The App may include optional consumable in-app purchases that let you support development.

These purchases do not unlock extra features. They only add stars on the main page of the App:

* USD 0.99 support purchase: adds 1 star
* USD 2.99 support purchase: adds 3 stars
* USD 4.99 support purchase: adds 5 stars

Stars from multiple purchases are added together on the device where they are stored.

Because stars are stored locally, they may not persist if the App is deleted or used on another device.

In-app purchase payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may process purchase-related information under Apple's own privacy policy and App Store terms.

## 6. No Ads or Tracking

We do not:

* Show ads
* Use advertising networks
* Track you across apps or websites
* Sell personal data
* Share personal data with data brokers
* Use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA)

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App's core chat functionality runs locally on your device.

We use Hugging Face to provide downloadable model files. Hugging Face does not receive your prompts, chat messages, or AI responses from the App.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, cloud database, or crash reporting services to collect data from the App.

Apple may provide App Store services such as downloads, updates, and in-app purchase processing. Those services are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## 8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has contacted us directly with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Contacting Support

If you email us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your email address, message, device details, screenshots, or other information you send voluntarily.

We use support emails only to respond to your request.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted in the App Store listing, the App, or our support website.
